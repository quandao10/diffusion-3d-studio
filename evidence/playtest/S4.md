## Observer notes

- **Facilitator hints needed: 0.** Everything was discoverable from the How-to-play box, the Challenge panel text and the Menu.
- **Phase timings:** onboarding plus leaderboard check 00:32–01:50 (about 1.3 min). Rule testing and prompt 01:50–03:00. Generation 1: 03:00–05:26 (about 2.4 min wall clock; the server reported 149 s elapsed). Photo shoot 1: 05:36–07:08 (about 1.5 min). Generation 2: 07:38–10:18 (about 2.7 min). Photo shoot 2: 10:43–12:38 (about 2 min). Leaderboard and questionnaire: 13:02–14:11. Total about 14 min.
- **Rules:** forbidden-word check is live and blocks Generate ("fox" and "foxes" blocked). **Loophole:** "F O X" with spaced letters is accepted (Generate stayed enabled). Not exploited.
- **Silent prompt truncation (most damaging bug for this player):** the 2nd prompt (~190 characters typed) was cut at ~120 characters ("…large black-tipp"), with no counter or warning. The cut-off was only visible afterwards in the textarea, the job line and the command log. It removed key fox features from the prompt.
- **Photo shoot made scores worse in both rounds.** Round 1: auto 43% → photos 28% (−15). Round 2: auto **51% fox = a winning preview** → photos 29% (−22), result 0 pts. "Keep auto-camera verdict" exists, but there is no live feedback on how the judge sees the current framing, so a player can't tell whether their photos will help. This is where the player hesitated (10:43) and lost.
- **Judge bias:** "corgi dog" was the main rival both times (57%/72%/70% in the losing verdicts). Any orange/white small canine gets pulled toward corgi.
- **Colour:** "red" in prompt 2 produced a magenta/pink model.
- **UI issues seen:** (1) Leaderboard "Close" returned to the Menu, not to the game (needed Esc). (2) The main 3D viewer kept showing the old owl example while my job trained; the live progress was only in the snapshot strip further down the page. (3) The Photo-shoot panel covers about the lower half of the 3D canvas, so the legs were hidden while framing (the captured photos did include them). (4) "STARTING" was shown together with "#1 in queue". (5) No visible confirmation after "Send answers" (the pop-up just closed).
- **Lag:** none noticeable. Renderer reported 16–24 FPS. Generations started immediately despite 3 other jobs running.
- **Scoring fairness:** the formula (100 × confidence × difficulty) is clearly stated, and the post-round breakdown (rank, auto vs photo %, confidence delta) is transparent. But it felt unfair that the final verdict came only from my photos when the auto preview had already identified the target. Better-of-both or a live judge readout would feel fairer.

## Post-play interview

(Same answers as submitted in the in-app form.)

1. **Most confusing/frustrating:** The photo shoot actively hurt me. Both rounds (fox, hard), my 3 photos LOWERED the judge's confidence compared with the automatic preview (43%→28%, then 51%→29%). In round 2 the preview already said fox (51%), so the photo shoot turned a win into 0 pts. Nothing tells you which angles the judge likes or warns you before you send. Also, the prompt box silently cut my 2nd prompt at ~120 characters ("large black-tipp") with no counter, so "pointed ears, muzzle, amber eyes" never reached the model.
2. **Intuitiveness of controls:** 7/10.
3. **Visual glitches or lag:** No real lag (renderer 16–21 FPS). The Photo-shoot panel covers the lower half of the 3D view, so I could not see the legs while framing (the photos did include them). The main viewer kept showing the old owl example while my model trained; the live progress was only in the snapshot strip lower down. "red" in my prompt came out magenta/pink.
4. **Fun:** 6/10.
5. **One change:** Show the judge's live guess (or at least the confidence) while framing in the photo shoot, or let me keep the better of auto-verdict vs my photos. Also show a character counter/limit on the prompt box.
