Setup: headless Chrome, window 1366x768 (visible page area about 1366x591), URL `?playtest=S2`. The facilitator only gave the premise; nothing else was explained.
Date: 2026-11-24, about 21:16–21:30 server-clock time. Timestamps are minutes:seconds after the page first loaded, taken when each action finished.
Generations submitted: 2 (both MVDream, all settings left at default).

## Observer notes

- **Facilitator hints needed: 0.**
- **Phase timings:**
  - Onboarding (read the How-to-play box, find the target, re-open Help): about 0:55.
  - Round 1: generation 01:04 → 03:57 (server "elapsed 175 s"; popup said optimisation 92.6 s, total 161.7 s). Photo shoot 04:08 → 05:35 (about 1.5 min).
  - Round 2: generation 06:20 → 09:15 (popup said optimisation 95.3 s, total 141.8 s). Photo shoot 09:35 → 11:15 (about 1.7 min).
  - Survey 11:37 → 13:41. Whole session about 14 min.
- **Hesitations and confusion:**
  - **Finding the target.** After "Let's play" the page auto-scrolled to the prompt box, so the target card was off-screen at first and I had to scroll up to find it.
  - **Help button.** "? Help" only re-shows the same How-to-play box. There is no explanation of the controls or the jargon.
  - **Technical terms.** Seed, Steps, CFG scale, "Guidance (score distillation)" (MVDream/SDS/ISM/NFSD/CSD), SDS loss, gradient norm, Gaussians, "Prompt match (CLIP)" and peak VRAM were all left unexplained. The terminal log box and a long PyTorch warning appeared during loading and looked like an error to me.
  - **"Prompt match (CLIP): 22.16 (good results score about 25–30)"** looked like a bad grade, even though the judge was 91% sure and I won. It's a mixed message.
  - **"Keep auto verdict" vs. "Start the photo shoot".** I took the photo shoot both times because the text says the final verdict comes from my photos. Nothing warned me that my photos could do worse than the auto verdict.
  - **Leaderboard Close** returned me to the main menu, not to the running round. I found Escape by trying it. There was no visible close or back-to-game button in the menu area I could see.
  - **Mouse wheel over the 3D view zooms the model instead of scrolling the page** (06:48). This zoom carried over into the next photo shoot (09:35), so the new model started extremely close up and edge-on.
- **Main usability problem: the photo-shoot panel covers the 3D model.** In both shoots the "Photo shoot" box sat over the centre of the renderer, and only a strip about 80 px tall above it showed the model (see S2_photoshoot_panel_covers_model.png). I framed shots blind and judged them only from the 50 px thumbnails. The photos themselves were not blocked by the panel.
- **Outcome of the photo shoots:**
  - Round 1 (crown): auto camera 91% → my photos 92% (+1).
  - Round 2 (sailboat): auto camera 41% → my photos 3% (−37). The judge said crown, so it went from a likely win to a loss.
- **Visual glitches and lag:**
  - **Broken snapshot image.** Just after Generate (01:15), the snapshot strip showed a broken-image icon with alt text "training snapshot strip"; real snapshots appeared later (by 02:55).
  - **Overlapping badge.** "MEDIUM ×1.5" overlaps "round N · N wins" on the challenge card the whole time.
  - **Playtest bar overlap.** The purple playtest bar sits over the lower part of the renderer controls (e.g. the reset view / Save image row during the photo shoot) and the bottom edge of the popups.
  - **FPS readings** seen in the renderer overlay (561×364 canvas):
    - 20 FPS on the example owl (220k Gaussians)
    - 15, 14, 19, 18, 20, 21 FPS during the crown shoot (260k Gaussians)
    - 21 FPS after round 1
    - 11 FPS when the sailboat shoot opened, then 20, 19, 21 (341k Gaussians)
    - GPU ms between 2.89 and 8.07, sort 5–9 ms
- **Errors:** no error dialog appeared. The only error-like text was the PyTorch `torch.load` warning in the log box during "LOADING DIFFUSION MODEL".
- **Survey oddity:** after I typed the five answers, field 5 contained the text of answer 1 with my answer 5 inserted in the middle. The cause is unknown; it could also have been my own click and typing. I cleared and retyped it. After "Send answers" the dialog closed without a confirmation message that I could see.
- **Things I didn't use:** the light direction/height sliders, the "studio lighting" checkbox, "reset view", Save image, .ply, the difficulty selector, New target, Explore in 3D, Exhibition and Gallery. I never changed any generation settings.
