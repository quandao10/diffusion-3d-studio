## Post-play interview

(Same answers as entered in the in-app form.)

1. **Most confusing or frustrating part:** On my phone the "Photo shoot" panel sits on top of the 3D view and covers almost all of it, so I could barely see the model while framing. I rotated it blind and judged from the tiny thumbnails. The purple Playtest bar also covers the buttons at the bottom of the result pop-ups (I tapped a sliver of an "Ex…" button without knowing what it was), and the pop-ups would not scroll. My first tap on "Send 3 photos to the judge" seemed to do nothing.
2. **Intuitiveness of the controls:** 5/10.
3. **Visual glitches or lag:** The viewer ran about 16–22 FPS on screen (33 FPS on one gallery owl), with GPU 1–10 ms, so orbiting felt choppy. In Exhibition with 8 models, the objects overlap and interpenetrate in one clump, and LOD 0.6M vs 1.2M looked the same at this canvas size. Splats show as coarse glittery discs (bike wheels, owl). Once, the bike view turned into a full-canvas red blur (camera too close) until I hit reset view. The FPS overlay text is cut off on the right and covers the top of the canvas.
4. **Fun of the Challenge:** 7/10.
5. **One change:** On phones, put the photo-shoot controls in a bar below the canvas instead of on top of it, make the canvas bigger, and keep the playtest bar from covering buttons.
